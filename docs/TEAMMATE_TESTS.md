# UI/UX test pass for T (about 40 minutes)

You do not need to know farming. The question in every step is: **could a stranger understand and use
this without anyone explaining it?** Write every problem as one row in `docs/BUGS.md` (device, steps,
what happened, what you expected). A screenshot helps; send it to N.

Live link: https://nazariipolishchuk.github.io/lan/ — test on your phone first, then on a computer.
Use a private window the first time, so you see the intro as a new visitor.

## 1. First minute (phone)
1. Open the link. Read the intro screen. Can you say in one sentence what the site does?
2. Press "Show me how · 1 min". Go through the 9-step guide (map, field card, comparison). Did any step
   point at the wrong place, hide the thing it talks about, or run off the screen? Did you understand
   what to do next after the last step?
3. Open the link again and press "Find my field". Type a village you know (in Ukrainian and in
   Latin letters). Did it find it? Did the map fly there?

## 2. The map
4. Zoomed out: tap the chips "Latest season", "Drought", "Rotation". Is it clear what the colours
   mean from the legend alone? Is anything in the legend confusing?
5. Zoom in until fields appear. Tap a field. Hover the mouse over fields near the right and bottom
   edges of the screen (computer). Does anything jump or flicker?
6. Press the location button (◎). If you deny permission, is the message clear?

## 3. The field card
7. Read the card top to bottom. Mark every word or number you did not understand.
8. Tap every ⓘ. Does the note stay fully on screen? Does it explain the block in plain words?
9. Tap "Edit" in the history strip and change one year. Did the card change? Was it obvious how?
10. Open "Why?" and look at each chart. For each one: what is on the vertical axis, what is on the
    horizontal one, and what is the main message? If you cannot answer, that is a bug.

## 4. Compare rotations
11. Press "Compare rotations". Can you tell which plan is best and why, within 30 seconds?
12. In "My plan" pick crops for each season. Did the score change at once? Were greyed choices
    explained?

## 5. Everything else
13. Switch EN / UK and light / dark. Anything untranslated, unreadable or overlapping?
14. Open About. Is anything there that a judge would find hard to believe or hard to read?
15. Small buttons: was anything hard to hit with a finger?

Rate the whole thing 1–5 for "looks trustworthy" and 1–5 for "easy to use", and write the one thing
you would change first.
